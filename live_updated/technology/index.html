Enterprise AI Engineering

Accelerated Intelligence Group (AIG) is Quadra's Enterprise AI Engineering practice, focused on helping organizations move beyond isolated AI pilots to production-ready intelligent systems. We design, build, govern, secure, and scale AI solutions that become part of everyday business operations.

Built across Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud, AIG combines deep enterprise engineering, agentic AI, application modernization, governance, and security to help customers realize the full value of artificial intelligence.
