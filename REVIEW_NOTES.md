## What was changed in the review draft

### 1. Preserve the security boundary
You reconfirmed that CSG remains non-Microsoft and Microsoft Security belongs within ISPG. The new CSG document included Microsoft in its capability/vendor list, endpoint content and cloud-security scope. Those implementation assertions have been removed from CSG's draft. The shared **Secure Your Enterprise** route remains an outcome-led entry point, not a merged security practice. AIG's AI lifecycle governance is also distinct from cybersecurity controls.

### 2. Correct and qualify the technology claims
The evidence register records current product terms, edition/availability conditions and recommended changes. In the mockup:
- Microsoft Foundry and Agent 365 have distinct roles rather than being treated as interchangeable platforms.
- Google AI copy uses the current platform/runtime context without promising that every migration is disruption-free.
- Data Studio is distinguished from Looker; reporting is not described as the place where a data warehouse is built.
- Grounding, guardrails and access controls are described as engineering controls, not guarantees that hallucinations or leakage cannot occur.
- Fabric/Databricks interoperability is assessment-led; security and access policies are not assumed to carry over automatically.

The source-to-claim records and authoritative URLs are in `external_validation.md` and `external_validation.json`.

### 3. Remove unsupported universal promises
The practice drafts no longer present blanket zero downtime, automatic compliance, guaranteed cost/performance improvements, unrestricted free-service eligibility or uniform service hours as standard commitments. They retain the practical delivery steps, technology depth and engagement-specific dependencies.

Existing case-study titles, summaries and metrics in the representative Happy Customers sample are unchanged. They remain claims from the existing published cases, not newly verified results. The underlying library has not been edited or reclassified.

### 4. Preserve continuity without promising an unlimited service
The content connects assessment, design, implementation, governance and the transition into operations. Managed services and support remain scoped engagements; they are not automatically included forever. AIR’s revised innovation-engine description is recorded in the 30 October AIG update below.

### 5. Keep the website hierarchy intact
The four solution routes remain customer entry points. Platforms & IP stays within Solutions. The Technology Practices menu retains its six-block pattern, with Microsoft, AWS and Google Cloud alongside Cybersecurity, Hybrid Cloud & Digital Work and Enterprise AI Engineering.

The CSG draft's additional AI-security and advisory pages are represented as capability/offer subpages inside its existing practice block—not new global sections. The internal review anchors are not production URLs. No new `/cyber-security` migration has been implemented.
