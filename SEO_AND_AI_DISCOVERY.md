# Search and AI-discovery requirements retained during review

The mockup is internal and noindex. It must not be indexed as a duplicate of the production site. Its fragment-based page views are review navigation only, not the intended production URL or rendering architecture.

## Every eventual production page
- Maintain a stable content ID, selected canonical URL, page title, description, one principal heading and an intelligible hierarchy of sections.
- Describe the customer's problem, the actual capability or offer, the relevant practice/platform, evidence and a specific next step. Use natural terminology; do not repeat every brand slogan or stuff vendor keywords into every section.
- Give each practice, offer and proprietary asset one substantive home; connect it from the four solution routes and relevant implementation pages. Separate owned IP, delivery models, commercial offers and partner technology.
- Preserve existing URLs by default. A changed label is not itself a migration justification. New recommended CSG `/cyber-security` routes must be reconciled with existing `/cybersecurity` URLs in the actual inventory before any redirect plan is approved.
- Cross-link the distinct CSG cybersecurity and ISPG Microsoft Security destinations as needed without collapsing them. Keep AIG cross-platform and cross-model; vendor-specific depth remains discoverable under the platform practice.
- Social metadata and image/alt mappings should use actual approved assets when visuals are added. No image URLs or alt claims are invented in this image-placeholder mockup.

## Schema and crawlability
- Generate accurate Organization/WebSite and page-specific schema only from approved facts and the content actually visible on the production page. Validate overlap with Framer's global output.
- Happy Customers is a CollectionPage; a future ItemList must bind actual current case titles, URLs and rendered order. Do not fabricate ratings, review stars, counts or Article dates from the page name.
- Keep core text and case links available without operating search controls. Progressive loading must have a crawlable route to the remaining pages; each genuine pagination page needs its own chosen canonical.
- Avoid an unbounded indexable space of arbitrary search/filter combinations. Decide robots, canonical and noindex policies together; blocking crawling does not allow a crawler to read a noindex directive.
- Do not add unnecessary FAQs, thin industry variants or a special 'AI schema' purely to chase citations. Valid markup alone does not guarantee indexing, rich results or assistant citations.

## The Compass and public answer systems
- Use the same approved content and entities as the visible site. Keep canonical links, current vendor names, source date and availability states aligned.
- Segment content by page and meaningful headings, retaining the source relationship. Do not mix internal practice instructions, unsupported scope statements or confidential source material into the public retrieval corpus.
- Approved metrics remain attached to their individual case context. Preserve anonymised customer names. No answer should generalise a result into a universal promise.
- Link the answer to the actual relevant practice, offer or case page; use a clear no-evidence fallback when required. The review mockup contains no live retrieval service and has not tested Compass answers.
- Check representative questions after publication: cloud costs, AI pilots to production, workplace adoption, specialist cybersecurity versus Microsoft Security, relevant industry cases, and unsupported requests.
- Keep any existing AI-facing site summary consistent with the approved site; it does not replace crawlable pages and supported claims.

## Validation before the Framer build and launch
Reconcile actual components and CMS fields first, then map this modular content. Confirm the site's live URL inventory, responsive layout, keyboard navigation, heading order, image rights, internal links, form destinations, analytics/consent, and staging noindex behaviour. Preserve the DMG baseline and existing measurement process. Implementation tasks and claim approvals remain separate from copy approval.

Primary implementation guidance retained from the earlier page pack: Google Search Central, AI features and your website (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features); pagination guidance (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/pagination-and-incremental-page-loading); Schema.org CollectionPage (https://schema.org/CollectionPage) and ItemList (https://schema.org/ItemList). These are requirements to verify in production, not a claim that the current website fails them.
