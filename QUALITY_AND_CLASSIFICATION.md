# Quadra website — positioning and release standard

## The standard for every practice page

**Cloud, AI and Security are a connected customer proposition—not three isolated product catalogues.** Each page should begin with the customer's constraint or ambition, explain the outcome sought, make Quadra's role concrete and lead to a credible next step. Infrastructure and digital work are foundations and experiences through which that proposition becomes real; they do not need forced AI language in every paragraph.

### Page acceptance test

- **Customer stakes:** A visitor can recognise a business problem, decision or opportunity without knowing Quadra's internal group names.
- **Outcome:** The page explains what should improve and how success would be evaluated; it does not invent a result, ROI or universal guarantee.
- **Quadra contribution:** The visitor can distinguish assessment, architecture, implementation, integration, adoption, operation and commercial advice from an OEM's own product features.
- **Delivery depth:** Important dependencies, trade-offs, tangible outputs, approval points and operational hand-offs are visible where relevant.
- **Connected expertise:** Related outcomes and practices are linked without merging their ownership. Microsoft Security remains in ISPG; CSG remains the non-Microsoft practice. HCDW identity recovery is a workload capability, not a reassignment of identity/security ownership.
- **Evidence:** Customer proof, credentials and service commitments have owners, scope and publication permission. Vendor documentation validates the vendor's capability—not Quadra's tier, readiness, SLA or results.
- **Next step:** The CTA describes an available conversation or scoped engagement. A named assessment is not advertised as free, fixed-duration or ready to buy unless approved.
- **Discoverability:** Clear H1/H2 hierarchy, descriptive titles, useful summaries, stable URLs, relevant internal links and accurate schema support both readers and grounded AI discovery. No keyword stuffing or invented FAQ claims.

## Common classification

Assign a primary type and explicit relationships; do not promote a service name into a software product just because it has a distinctive brand.

- **Practice:** enduring specialist expertise and delivery responsibility.
- **Capability:** a reusable ability, such as application modernization, identity recovery or endpoint management.
- **Commercial offer:** a selectable, scoped engagement with customer inputs, outputs, exclusions, commercial terms and an accountable owner.
- **IP:** an owned reusable asset, software or framework with an evidenced ownership and maturity position. OEM platforms are not Quadra IP.
- **Delivery model:** how expertise and responsibility are engaged and handed over. Continuity carries intent, context and responsibility through agreed scope; it is not indefinite entitlement or a promise of identical personnel.
- **Proof asset:** an approved, traceable customer result, credential or demonstrator. Customer Zero is a validation approach; it becomes proof only through a documented, permitted example.
- **Experience venue/platform:** a physical or digital environment in which a visitor can explore, learn or test. The CoE floor themes do not create new solution routes.

A solution is the customer-facing combination of capabilities, offers, platforms and delivery needed to address an outcome; it is not an eighth competing inventory of named products. A service is the work delivered within that combination. A framework is a repeatable method or asset whose exact type depends on ownership and use.

### Existing decisions remain binding

- Keep the four outcome entry routes and the six-block technology mega-menu.
- Keep Platforms & IP under Solutions rather than creating a fifth outcome route.
- Preserve AIG's approved replacement copy and closed D-01–D-06 decisions, including the supplied AIR innovation-engine definition and BluForge IntelliGuard wording.
- Retain existing customer cases and their canonical identities; do not rewrite or manufacture proof to complete a page.
- Keep the homepage vendor-neutral while preserving named vendor/practice navigation and appropriate product naming on detail pages.

## Where HCDW fits

The reviewed HCDW pages connect resilient infrastructure, secure digital work and recoverable data/identity to business continuity and AI readiness. Partner detail explains architectural fit and limitations rather than declaring a universal winner. Recovery objectives, supported workloads and licensing are design inputs; they are not implied guarantees. Expanded copy is proposed website positioning grounded in the submitted priorities, not independent proof that every possible configuration is currently delivered.

## Review workflow

1. Practice submissions remain untouched and versioned.
2. Normalize priorities into the common content master, adding missing explanatory depth without inventing commercial or delivery facts.
3. Maintain product validation and unresolved owner facts separately.
4. Regenerate the simple preview and staging package from that master.
5. Review in protected staging; close material publication gates without blocking safe editorial progress.
6. Approve and deploy the exact tested production version through a separate release decision.

Only genuine human-input gaps go back to practices. Do not ask a practice to research a public product name when an OEM source can settle it; do not ask an OEM feature page to prove a Quadra commitment.
