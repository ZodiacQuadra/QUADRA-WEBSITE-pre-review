1. **One connected customer story.** Cloud. AI. Security. One Architect. now means workload-led infrastructure economics, useful employee experiences and tested data/identity recovery. Recovery is essential to trustworthy AI, but is not represented as sufficient to make a model safe.
2. **Complete capability depth without changing the practice map.** Four original IDs/parents remain; the hub has four blueprint pillars. Identity/data/AI resilience lives within the resilience workstream. Three substantive OEM detail pages sit under `hcdw-resilience`, reached through actual links/deep views, not non-functional tabs or new global navigation.
3. **Work that a customer can scope and evaluate.** Added dependency maps, RTO/RPO objectives, restore tests, identity recovery order, migration/rollback, whole-life economics, employee journeys, runbooks and acceptance evidence. All author additions are marked in coverage, not passed off as supplied facts or existing fixed service commitments.
4. **Confidence without guarantees.** Removed universal uptime, instant/clean recovery, any-device/network, zero-impact, automatic-compliance and financial/savings guarantees. Vendor features are technology candidates whose release, licence, workload, region and integration must be checked. SaaS infrastructure management is separated from customer responsibilities.
5. **Clear ownership and commercial boundaries.** Microsoft Security stays ISPG; non-Microsoft controls stay CSG; AI lifecycle/governance stays AIG. HCDW supplies infrastructure/workspace/recovery context. Assessments are scoped commercial engagements; managed delivery is an operating model; OEM platforms are not Quadra IP. Governed handoff replaces fixed staff continuity.
