Quadra's 25-Year Legacy A History of Seeing What's Next

For 25 years, our journey has been defined by one core principle: looking beyond the immediate horizon to architect what's next. Our annual calendars have served as a public record of our evolving perspective—a year-by-year chronicle of our commitment to guiding our partners through every major technology shift, from the dawn of the cloud to the era of AI.

More Than a Calendar. It's a Compass.

Since our early days, we have used our annual calendar as more than just a marketing tool. It has been our public thesis—a declaration of the strategic themes we believe will define the coming year and a compass to guide our partners' thinking.

This visual history is the ultimate proof of our enduring philosophy. It shows that we don't just react to change; we anticipate it. We don't just follow trends; we help our partners architect a strategic response to them. From championing virtualization during the financial crisis to advocating for a data-first approach long before it was mainstream, this timeline demonstrates our long-term commitment to your long-term success.

Explore our journey below and see the evolution of the ideas that have shaped our industry and the blueprints we've used to architect our partners' ambition.
