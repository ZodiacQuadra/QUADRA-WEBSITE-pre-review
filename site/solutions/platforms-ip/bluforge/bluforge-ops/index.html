BluForge Ops

24/7 Managed SOC & Security Operations

BluForge Ops™ is your fully managed, 24x7 Security Operations Center. We provide real-time threat detection, triage, and management using our powerful, AI-driven platform, giving you enterprise-grade vigilance without the high cost and complexity of building your own SOC.

The Unwinnable Race Against Time

A modern threat landscape never sleeps, but your team has to. Building and staffing a 24x7 SOC is prohibitively expensive and requires a constant, competitive search for scarce expert talent. How can you ensure continuous, expert-led monitoring without breaking your budget?

Your Team, Extended. Your Defense, Uninterrupted.

We act as a seamless extension of your team. Our blueprint for managed SOC is built on three core principles

AI-Powered Platform

We leverage a platform built on Microsoft Sentinel and Defender, using its native AI and automation to correlate trillions of signals into prioritized, high-fidelity incidents.

Human-Led Expertise

Our team of Microsoft-certified analysts provides the crucial layer of human intelligence. They manage the platform, triage every alert, and orchestrate containment 24x7x365.

Complete Transparency

You receive monthly executive reports, custom dashboards, and continuous monitoring of all environments, giving you complete visibility into your security posture.
