GOOGLE CLOUD · ARCHITECTURE TO OPERATION

Create the data foundation that better decisions need

Siloed data slows reporting and limits useful AI. Quadra unifies data on Google Cloud, governs its use and makes it available to analytics and AI workloads through a deliberate architecture.

Design your Google Cloud data platform
01

Unify, govern, activate

Ingest and migrate data into BigQuery using managed pipelines and transfer services. Establish quality, catalogue, lineage and access policies. Then connect self-service reporting, conversational analysis and machine learning to the same governed foundation.

02

Engineer the platform

  • Warehouse modernisation: assess legacy Teradata, Oracle, Snowflake and Hadoop/Hive workloads for a suitable BigQuery transition.
  • Batch and streaming data: build pipelines with Dataflow, Pub/Sub and supported database or SaaS connectors.
  • Analytics: design governed dashboards and business-facing insight with an appropriate semantic and access model.
  • AI-ready access: make relevant data available to approved applications and agents without bypassing permissions.
  • Governance and quality: connect catalogue, lineage, quality checks and fine-grained access across the estate.
  • Cost optimisation: review reservations, autoscaling and query behaviour against usage needs.
03

Match technology to the data problem

BigQuery, Data Transfer Service, Dataflow, Pub/Sub, Dataproc, BigLake and governance services form a toolkit, not a prescribed bundle. Business definitions, source-system readiness and workload characteristics shape the implementation.

04

Keep the platform useful after launch

Agree measures for information freshness, quality, access and query performance, and review operating costs against the baseline. Connect pipeline and platform management to ongoing operations. Maintain business definitions and data ownership as sources and analytics requirements evolve.

05

Keep platform roles clear

BigQuery provides the data-platform foundation; Data Studio supports reporting and visualisation. Data Studio is the renamed Looker Studio product, not a new name for Looker. Select the reporting and semantic approach for the actual governance and analysis need.
