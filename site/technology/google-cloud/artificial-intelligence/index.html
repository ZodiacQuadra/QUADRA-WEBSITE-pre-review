GOOGLE CLOUD · ARCHITECTURE TO OPERATION

Turn a promising AI use case into a working service

Build enterprise AI on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, the evolution of Vertex AI. Quadra connects model choice, enterprise data, integration and evaluation to the workflow you need to change.

Discuss your Google Cloud AI use case
01

Ideate, build, scale

Use an outcome-focused workshop to select priority use cases, business owners and success measures. Build against relevant enterprise data and systems; evaluate before release; then expand with monitoring, governance and cost visibility.

02

Choose the right engineering pattern

  • Custom agents: single- or multi-agent designs where the workflow benefits from contextual reasoning and coordinated tasks.
  • Workflow integration: connect approved systems and tools, with access boundaries and rollout controls.
  • Enterprise retrieval and search: use retrieval-augmented generation and vector search to provide relevant business context.
  • Bespoke machine learning: forecasting, classification and anomaly detection suited to the data and decision.
  • AI-assisted development: evaluate suitable development support within the team’s engineering controls.
  • Model optimisation: select and review models against quality, latency, policy and cost requirements.
03

Build trust through evaluation

Evaluate representative tasks, ground responses in relevant enterprise information and apply safety and access controls. Make review and escalation part of the production workflow. Spend reporting, quotas and model routing help teams understand usage and manage cost as adoption grows.

04

Work with the estate you have

Review existing Google Cloud AI workloads before changing platform components. Google, third-party and open-model options depend on service availability and workload fit. Handover should preserve the evaluation baseline, integration context and operating responsibilities so the service can evolve after launch.

05

Platform depth, with migration discipline

Use the platform’s model, grounding, evaluation and Agent Runtime capabilities where they fit the workload. Review existing SDKs, namespaces and dependencies before migration rather than assuming a product rename requires no application changes.
