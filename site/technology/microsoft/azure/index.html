MICROSOFT AZURE

Build the intelligent enterprise on Microsoft Azure

Turn cloud investment into a platform for what comes next. Quadra connects infrastructure, applications, databases, data, AI and security on Azure, from the first architecture decision to ongoing operation.

Architect your Azure roadmapExplore this area
01

One architecture, from ambition to operation

A data-centre exit, application refresh or AI initiative should strengthen the wider estate. We assess dependencies and business priorities, establish the foundation, and carry design context into implementation and managed operations.

Choose your starting point

01

Migration and modernisation

Assess workloads, establish landing zones and sequence infrastructure, SAP, VMware, database and hybrid transitions.

02

Applications and engineering

Modernise applications and APIs, automate delivery with GitHub and build cloud-native services.

03

Data, analytics and AI

Connect Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, SQL and Microsoft Foundry to governed business data and useful decisions.

04

Security and governance

Embed identity, policy, threat detection, data protection and cost accountability into the Azure design.

05

Managed services

Operate infrastructure, apps, databases, data platforms and AI, with monitoring and an improvement roadmap.

03

Built around business context

Manufacturing may start with plant and quality intelligence; BFSI with risk and resilience; pharma and healthcare with governed information; retail with inventory and customer insight; IT/ITES with developer productivity and service operations. The workload determines the architecture.

04

Continuity by design

Cloud Adoption Framework and Well-Architected practices guide the design. Assessments, runbooks and automation support handover, while the agreed operating model defines scope, responsibilities and success measures. Explore customer work in Happy Customers.
