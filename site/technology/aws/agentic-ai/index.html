AWS · AWS AGENTIC AI

Agentic AI

From AI Assistance to Governed Business Action

Quadra helps organizations move beyond standalone chatbots and assistants by building secure, context-aware AI agents capable of understanding goals, using approved tools, and coordinating multi-step actions. Agents operate within defined permissions, approval requirements and monitoring controls.

Talk to an ArchitectExplore this area
01

1. Identify High-Value Agentic AI Opportunities

Organizations often begin AI initiatives without clearly identifying the right business problem, process owner, expected value, or governance requirements, resulting in fragmented experimentation and limited adoption.

Quadra assesses business processes to identify and prioritize Agentic AI opportunities based on process complexity, manual effort, data availability, automation potential, business risk, and expected value.

Outcomes to work toward: Focused AI investment, faster time to value, reduced experimentation risk, and stronger alignment with business priorities.

02

2. Build Intelligent Agents Connected to Enterprise Knowledge and Systems

Business processes require more than information retrieval. Employees need to find trusted information, understand business context, interact with applications, and coordinate activities across multiple enterprise systems.

Quadra builds intelligent agents that connect with authorized enterprise knowledge, applications, APIs, databases, and business tools to reason, maintain context, support decisions, and execute governed actions. Tool use and system changes stay within authorized permissions and required human approvals.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster knowledge access, reduced system switching, improved decision-making, reduced manual coordination, and scalable business process automation.

03

3. Automate Complex Business Workflows with Agentic AI

Complex enterprise processes often involve multiple activities such as document analysis, validation, policy checks, exception handling, approvals, communications, and system updates, creating manual handoffs and operational delays.

Quadra designs coordinated agentic workflows where specialized agents work together across different tasks while retaining human approvals for high-risk, regulated, or business-critical decisions. Tool use and system changes stay within authorized permissions and required human approvals.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster process execution, fewer manual handoffs, improved workflow consistency, reduced operational effort, and greater end-to-end automation.

04

4. Accelerate Employee Productivity and Software Engineering

Business and technology teams spend significant time searching for information, preparing reports, creating content, updating systems, developing applications, testing, maintaining documentation, and performing repetitive activities.

Quadra introduces AI-powered and agentic experiences across business and engineering workflows, helping employees access knowledge, analyze information, create business deliverables, initiate workflows, develop applications, test solutions, and automate repetitive activities. Tool use and system changes stay within authorized permissions and required human approvals.

Outcomes to work toward: Higher employee and developer productivity, shorter development cycles, faster access to information, reduced repetitive work, and increased focus on higher-value activities.

05

5. Modernize Applications and Operationalize Agentic AI Securely

Organizations want to introduce Agentic AI into existing business applications and processes without rebuilding their entire technology landscape, while maintaining security, governance, control, and visibility.

Quadra introduces intelligent agentic capabilities around existing applications and workflows with appropriate identity, access, permissions, human approvals, monitoring, governance, and lifecycle controls for enterprise adoption.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster AI adoption, extended value from existing technology investments, reduced modernization effort, controlled automation, lower operational risk, and scalable Agentic AI adoption.
