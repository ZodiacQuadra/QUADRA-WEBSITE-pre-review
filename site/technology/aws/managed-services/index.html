AWS · AWS MANAGED SERVICES | MAX-IT

Managed Services | Max-IT

Intelligent Cloud Operations for Continuous Business Value

Quadra combines cloud operations, cloud expertise, automation, Agentic AI, security, resilience, and cost management to help customers operate AWS environments reliably and efficiently. Monitoring, response, escalation and authorized actions are defined by the agreed service scope.

Talk to an Architect
01

CloudOps

Cloud operations teams can be overwhelmed by alert volumes, repetitive incidents, manual patching, and inconsistent operational processes.

Quadra provides monitoring, incident response, patching, backup management, operational governance and approved automated remediation within the agreed service scope. Coverage, service levels, response targets and escalation procedures are agreed for the supported environment.

Outcomes to work toward: Higher availability, lower operational effort, faster incident response and more predictable service delivery.

02

DevOps

Manual development and deployment processes can create slow releases, inconsistent environments and frequent deployment failures.

Quadra establishes automated build, test, release, infrastructure provisioning and deployment practices that improve delivery consistency.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster releases, reliable deployments, consistent environments and improved developer productivity.

03

DevSecOps

Security issues discovered late in the development lifecycle are more expensive and disruptive to resolve.

Quadra integrates security checks into development and deployment workflows, covering code, dependencies, containers, infrastructure definitions, secrets and access controls.

Outcomes to work toward: Earlier risk detection, fewer production vulnerabilities, faster secure releases and consistent security guardrails.

04

SecOps

Security teams frequently face fragmented alerts, slow investigations and limited visibility across accounts and workloads.

Quadra delivers security monitoring within agreed coverage, risk prioritization, vulnerability management, incident coordination, compliance tracking, and remediation guidance.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster threat response, reduced exposure, improved security posture and stronger audit readiness.

05

FinOps and Cost Optimization

Cloud costs can grow without clear ownership, usage visibility, budget controls or continuous optimization.

Quadra monitors cloud consumption, investigates unusual spending, identifies idle and oversized resources, evaluates commitment options and provides actionable recommendations at an agreed cadence.

Outcomes to work toward: Reduced cloud waste, improved budget predictability, clearer cost accountability, and better return on cloud investment.

06

Intelligent FinOps Automation

Cost reviews are often retrospective and dependent on manual analysis.

Quadra applies AI-assisted cost analysis to detect anomalies, investigate cost drivers, identify optimization opportunities, and automate recurring FinOps workflows.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster anomaly investigation, continuous savings identification, improved cost accountability and reduced manual analysis.

07

AI-Powered Operations

Traditional monitoring tools can generate large volumes of isolated alerts without providing sufficient context.

Quadra uses AI-driven operational capabilities to correlate events, identify abnormal behavior, support root-cause analysis, recommend remediation, and automate approved recovery actions.

Outcomes to work toward: Reduced alert noise, faster diagnosis, shorter resolution times, improved reliability and lower operational effort.

08

Automated Insights and Reporting

Manual reporting can be delayed, inconsistent and difficult to scale across multiple cloud accounts.

Quadra automates operational assessments, infrastructure inventories, risk summaries, cost insights and prioritized action reports.

Outcomes to work toward: Consistent reporting, improved visibility, faster decision-making, and clearer ownership of operational actions.

09

Automated Architecture Assessment

Architecture reviews can be time-consuming and may rely on incomplete technical evidence.

Quadra uses an in-house assessment accelerator to evaluate cloud environments against architecture best practices across operations, security, reliability, performance, cost and sustainability.

Outcomes to work toward: Faster assessments, evidence-based findings, prioritized remediation, and a practical improvement roadmap.

10

Backup and Disaster Recovery

Backups that are not governed or tested may not provide effective protection during an actual disruption.

Quadra manages backup policies, retention, recovery readiness, cross-account or cross-region protection and regular recovery testing aligned with business requirements.

Outcomes to work toward: Improved recovery confidence, reduced data-loss risk, validated resilience, and stronger business continuity.

11

Strategic Advisory

Cloud environments require continuous alignment with changing business priorities, risk requirements and technology capabilities.

Quadra provides ongoing architecture, modernization, operations, security, FinOps and resilience guidance based on actual usage and business goals.

Outcomes to work toward: Better investment decisions, sustained modernization, improved governance and a cloud roadmap aligned with business growth.
