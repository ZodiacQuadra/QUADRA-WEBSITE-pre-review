ENTERPRISE AI ENGINEERING · AIG

Engineering the Agentic Enterprise

Enterprise AI Engineering. From Strategy to Enterprise Scale.

Most organizations have experimented with AI. Few have successfully transformed it into measurable business outcomes.

Accelerated Intelligence Group (AIG) is Quadra's Enterprise AI Engineering practice, focused on helping organizations move beyond isolated AI pilots to production-ready intelligent systems. We design, build, govern, secure, and scale AI solutions that become part of everyday business operations.

Built across Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud, AIG combines deep enterprise engineering, agentic AI, application modernization, governance, and security to help customers realize the full value of artificial intelligence.

Why Enterprise AI Fails

AI adoption is growing rapidly, yet many initiatives struggle to create lasting business impact.

The challenge is rarely the model itself. The real barriers are:

  • Disconnected enterprise systems
  • Lack of governance and security
  • Limited business process integration
  • Architecture not designed for AI
  • Failure to scale beyond proofs of concept

AIG exists to close this gap by connecting AI directly to enterprise workflows, systems of record, people, and governance frameworks.
