CYBERSECURITY · PRACTICE

Best-of-breed security. One accountable defence.

Connect security investment to a coherent defence. Quadra’s Cybersecurity Solutions Group architects, integrates and operates non-Microsoft security controls across access, endpoint, network, cloud, applications, data and AI.

Discuss your cyber defence blueprintExplore this area
01

Turn fragmented tools into coordinated protection

Distributed users, changing cloud estates and disconnected alerts create operational gaps. We align technology selection, integration, response responsibilities and governance to the risks that matter to your organisation.

The Cyber Defence Blueprint

01

Zero Trust and SASE

Give users context-aware access to authorised applications, with data controls and experience monitoring.

02

Endpoint and identity defence

Connect EDR, XDR, identity telemetry, threat hunting and containment workflows.

03

Network, email, applications and APIs

Layer inspection, authentication, protection and policy across common entry points.

04

Cloud-native security

Connect posture, entitlements, workload and pipeline controls from code to runtime.

05

AI security

Map AI usage and data exposure, then establish practical access and protection controls.

06

Managed operations

Monitor, investigate, tune detections and coordinate authorised response under a defined operating model.

07

Risk and advisory

Prioritise exposure, readiness gaps and investment through evidence-led assessment.

03

Architecture through operations

Assess the current state; design control mappings and high- and low-level architectures; implement and test; then manage and govern through agreed reviews. Customer-led, co-managed and managed approaches define who decides, who acts and who retains remediation responsibility.

04

Specialist depth without disconnected ownership

Zscaler, CrowdStrike, Sophos, Fortinet and Barracuda bring complementary controls to the architecture. Platform selection follows business risk and integration needs. For Microsoft-specific security, explore our separate Microsoft Security practice. Discover customer work in Happy Customers.

01

Zero Trust & SASE

Replace perimeter-dependent access with identity-, device- and context-aware controls. Quadra uses Zscaler and complementary non-Microsoft technologies to connect authorised users to applications while reducing unnecessary exposure.

Endpoint, Identity & Threat Defence

Ransomware and credential misuse rarely stay inside one console. Quadra connects endpoint, identity, network and cloud telemetry using CrowdStrike, Sophos and complementary non-Microsoft platforms.

Explore03

Network, Email, Application & API Security

Phishing, exposed services, vulnerable applications and API abuse can form one attack chain. Quadra brings Fortinet, Barracuda and complementary non-Microsoft controls into a layered architecture.

Explore04

Cloud-Native Security / CNAPP

Bring cloud posture, identity, workload and development controls into a shared risk view. Quadra designs CNAPP architectures using Fortinet, CrowdStrike and complementary non-Microsoft controls for hybrid and multicloud estates.

Explore05

AI Security & Control Governance

AI use can spread faster than security policies. Quadra helps organisations understand where AI touches users, applications and sensitive data, then design controls for leakage, misuse and unauthorised access.

Managed Security Operations — BluForge
