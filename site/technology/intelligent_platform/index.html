Solutions for Enterprise Productivity

We architect and build the custom AI solutions and intelligent platforms that redefine how work gets done. From extending Copilot to building multi-agent systems, we engineer the AI-powered capabilities that unlock your enterprise's true potential

Off-the-Shelf AI Solves Generic Problems. Your Ambition is Not Generic

To gain a true competitive edge, you need AI that understands your business, your data, and your unique workflows

How do I make generative AI speak my business language?

Off-the-shelf AI doesn't know your customers, products, or processes. You need custom intelligence that is securely connected to your systems of record.

How do we move from simple automation to true intelligence?

Traditional automation is rigid. You need adaptive, AI-powered platforms that can reason, learn, and handle the dynamic nature of modern business challenges.

How can AI solve complex, multi-step business problems?

Most AI assistants are task-oriented. You need intelligent systems that can orchestrate complex processes and collaborate across departments, just like your best teams do.

How do we empower our teams to build with AI without creating chaos?

You need a framework for "citizen development" that fosters innovation while maintaining enterprise-grade security, governance, and control.

The Blueprint for Applied Enterprise Intelligence

We don't just connect you to AI; we architect it into the core of your business. Our approach transforms AI from a tool into a powerful, integrated capability

Microsoft Copilot Extensibility

Microsoft Copilot Extensibility: We are experts in Copilot Studio, building both custom declarative and Procode-based agents that securely connect Copilot to your enterprise data, understand your unique workflows, and automate real business logic.

Agentic AI Systems with Intellient

Built on frameworks like Microsoft AutoGen, Intellient is Quadra’s proprietary platform for creating autonomous, collaborative AI agents that orchestrate knowledge work and solve complex, multi-step problems.

Intelligent Application Development & App Modernization

We build custom, AI-native business applications on the hyperscaler platforms of your choice—Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud—while modernizing your existing applications and transforming your core processes with intelligent automation

Enterprise Power Platform Solutions
