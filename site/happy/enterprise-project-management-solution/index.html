Architecting an Enterprise Project Management Platform for a Global Engineering Leader

Client

A world leader in electrical engineering and software with nearly 20,000 employees operating across 60 countries.

Ambition

To unify their project management processes and tools onto a single, enterprise-wide platform to drive efficiency, reduce risk, and enhance quality.

Outcome

Architected and deployed a standardized Enterprise Project Management (EPM) platform that delivered deep visibility into operations, enhanced efficiency, and improved employee satisfaction.

The Ambition

A world leader in electrical engineering and software with nearly 20,000 employees operating across 60 countries.

The Challenge

Their existing project management landscape was fragmented. Over time, they had adopted a multitude of disparate tools and solutions. Instead of contributing to efficiency, this fragmentation was causing delays, creating information silos, and making it impossible to get a unified view of resource allocation and project status. The multitude of tools was causing the opposite of the intended effect.

The Architectural Blueprint

For a global business of this scale, the ambition was to achieve operational excellence. Efficient project management was a vital cog in their operations, as even the smallest delay could set off a chain reaction with organization-wide ramifications. They needed a single source of truth to manage hundreds of projects in real time.

Ambition, Realized

By standardizing on a single platform, we delivered deep visibility and powerful insights into their global operations. The solution greatly enhanced efficiency and employee satisfaction, with automated alerts to quickly identify deviations and escalations. Team members could now update timesheets from any device, reducing manual effort. The end-to-end solution, which included everything from architecture to end-user training, allowed them to realize significant business benefits without being encumbered by a complex deployment.

Key Business Impact

Standardized Project Management on a Single Global Platform

Delivered Deep Visibility & Insights into Operations

Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Productivity

Improved Resource Planning & Allocation
