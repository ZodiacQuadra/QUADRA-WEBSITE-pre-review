Architecting a Cost-Effective ERP Rollout for a National Apparel Brand

Client

A high-quality cotton apparel brand with a unique niche and an unwavering focus on ethical business practices.

Ambition

To roll out a new, business-critical ERP system without undertaking a massive, cost-prohibitive refresh of their entire legacy desktop infrastructure.

Outcome

Architected an innovative Hosted Shared Desktop (HSD) solution that enabled a speedy ERP rollout, saved significant capital expenditure, and extended the life of their existing IT investments.

The Ambition

A high-quality cotton apparel brand with a unique niche and an unwavering focus on ethical business practices.

The Challenge

The primary roadblock to their ERP deployment was their legacy desktop infrastructure. The existing PCs did not meet the minimum system requirements to run the new ERP client. The obvious solution—a full hardware refresh—was not a strategic use of capital, as most users only performed simple, routine tasks and did not need powerful new machines.

The Architectural Blueprint

The client's ambition was to modernize their core business processes with a new, market-leading ERP system to better manage their growth. They needed a way to achieve this critical business goal without derailing their budget with a secondary, multi-crore investment in new desktop hardware.

Ambition, Realized

The HSD solution was a huge financial and operational success. The client was able to roll out their critical ERP application on schedule, leveraging their existing desktop investments for several more years. This saved them significant capital expenditure which could be channeled into other strategic business initiatives. The solution also provided the added benefit of secure, 'any-device' access for their remote workforce, balancing productivity and security perfectly.

Key Business Impact

Saved Significant Capital by Avoiding a Desktop Refresh

Accelerated a Mission-Critical ERP Deployment

Extended the Lifespan of Existing IT Investments

Enabled Secure 'Work from Anywhere' Application Access
